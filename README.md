# Dress-Up Learning Games

Tap-to-play learning games for preschoolers. The buttons are big and chunky, and the game says every color, shape, number and letter out loud.

**▶ Play Zahra's Dress-Up: https://wkotlewski.github.io/Dress-Up-Learning-Games-/**

## Zahra's Dress-Up (`index.html`)

A dress-up game for a girl turning 4 who is learning colors, shapes, numbers, how to spell her name, and good manners.

### One step at a time

Each screen shows only the next choice, and the game reads it out loud. A big **Back** button and a **Home** button are always at the top, and the top bar shows where she is.

1. **Where are we going today?** She picks a place.
2. **In that place**, she gets dressed in a matching outfit. She can pick something to play, or choose what to change: tops, bottoms, shoes, hats, and so on. Only the clothes that fit that place show up.
3. **Pick a top** shows only the tops. She taps one and it goes on.
4. **Pick a color!** shows colors and patterns for that one thing. Then she taps **Done**.

She can also tap the girl herself: her head for hats, her chest for tops, her legs for bottoms, her feet for shoes, her face for makeup, or her hands to go to the nail salon.

### Places and things to do

| Place | Clothes | Let's play |
|---|---|---|
| Rainy day | raincoat, rain boots, rain hat, umbrella | Jump in puddles · Open the umbrella (count to five and a rainbow appears) |
| Sunny park | T-shirt, shorts, cap, sunglasses, bubble wand | Fly a kite (tap to change its color) · Blow and pop bubbles |
| Fancy party | party dress, ball gown, tiara, pearls, purse | Dance · Twirl |
| Ballet show | leotard, tutu, tights, ballet slippers | Dance on stage · Take a bow (flowers fly) |
| Soccer practice | jersey with a number, shorts, soccer socks, cleats | Kick the ball into the goal · Cheer |
| Bedtime | pajamas, nightgown, slippers, sleepy cap, sleep mask, teddy | Read a book with Teddy (the pages count 1–5 and spell Z‑A‑H‑R‑A) · Go to sleep (lights off, Zzz) |
| Beach | swimsuit, swim ring, flip-flops, sun hat, sand bucket | Swim and splash · Build a sandcastle one piece at a time, counting |
| My room | every piece of clothing in the game | Dance · Twirl |

There's also **Makeup** (lip gloss, cheeks, eye shadow, sticker tattoos) and **Me** (skin tone, hairstyle, hair color). She starts as a mixed-race girl with brown skin and dark curly hair.

### Nail salon

When she taps **Nails**, the game asks if she wants to go to the nail salon. There, Nina the nail artist asks what to do next, and Zahra chooses: soak (pop the bubbles), shape (short, long, square or pointy "fake" nails), polish, sparkles, dry (count to five), lotion, or hand tattoos. Each step she finishes gets a checkmark.

### Learning and manners

- **Questions** pop up now and then before something new goes on: "Which one is pink?", "How many stars?", "What number is on your jersey?", "How many pink nails?", "Which one is the heart?", "Z, A, H… what comes next?" She can tap the answer or press **Say it!** and say it out loud. A wrong answer tells her what she picked and lets her try again.
- **Please and thank you:** at the salon, Nina offers each step and Zahra answers **"Yes, please!"** or **"No, thank you."** She finishes each step with **"Thank you, Nina!"** and Nina says "You're welcome!"
- **Clean-up time:** after she plays somewhere, leaving brings up the toys on the floor. She taps each one to put it away in the toy box, counting as she goes, "so we don't lose them."

### For grown-ups

The gear button turns questions off, some or lots, turns clean-up time on or off, turns the voice on or off, and starts over. Her outfit is saved in the browser between visits. Answering out loud needs a browser that supports speech recognition, like Chrome, or Safari on iPhone and iPad. It asks for the microphone the first time.

### Run it

Play it at **https://wkotlewski.github.io/Dress-Up-Learning-Games-/** on any phone, tablet or computer. On an iPad or iPhone, tap Share → Add to Home Screen to give it its own icon.

The link works once GitHub Pages is on for this repo (Settings → Pages → deploy from `main`, root folder). The game is a single HTML file with no build step, so you can also open `index.html` straight in a browser.
