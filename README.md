# Dress-Up Learning Games

Tap-to-play learning games for preschoolers. The buttons are big and chunky, and the game says every color, shape, number and letter out loud.

**▶ Play Zahra's Dress-Up: https://wkotlewski.github.io/Dress-Up-Learning-Games-/**

## Zahra's Dress-Up (`index.html`)

A dress-up game for a girl turning 4 who is learning colors, shapes, numbers and how to spell her name.

### Play

- **Dress** – a full-body girl to dress. Tap part of her body to jump to the right clothes: head for hats and hair things, chest for tops and coats, legs for bottoms and socks, feet for shoes. Or use the buttons: tops, bottoms, coats, socks, shoes, hats, hair bows and clips, jewelry, and toys. Every item can be any of 10 colors and 7 patterns (plain, dots, stripes, stars, hearts, sparkle, checks).
- **Themes** – one tap dresses her as a **ballerina**, **soccer player**, **cowgirl**, **dinosaur** or **teddy bear**, with a matching place behind her (stage, soccer field, ranch, jungle, cozy bedroom).
- **Face** – a close-up of her face for lip gloss, cheeks, eye shadow and kid-friendly sticker tattoos (hearts, stars, butterflies, rainbows, flowers, dinosaurs, paw prints, gems).
- **Nails** – both hands up close. Pick short, long, square or pointy "fake" nails in any color or pattern (including sparkle), then tap each nail. The game counts as she goes. Sticker tattoos work on the hands too.
- **Me** – choose skin tone, hairstyle (curly, puffs, long curls, braids, top puff, bun) and hair color. It starts as a mixed-race girl with brown skin and dark curly hair.

### Learning questions

Every so often, when she puts something on, a question pops up first:

- **Colors** – "Which one is pink?" or "What color is this?"
- **Numbers** – "How many stars?", "What number is on your jersey?", "How many pink nails?"
- **Shapes** – "Which one is the heart?"
- **Her name** – "Z, A, H… what comes next?"

She can tap the answer or press **Say it!** and answer out loud. A wrong tap tells her what she picked and lets her try again. After two misses, the right answer starts to bounce. Once she gets it right, the new thing goes on.

### For grown-ups

The gear button sets how often questions appear (off, some, lots), turns the voice on or off, and starts over. Her outfit is saved in the browser between visits. Answering out loud needs a browser that supports speech recognition, like Chrome, or Safari on iPhone and iPad. It asks for the microphone the first time.

### Run it

Play it at **https://wkotlewski.github.io/Dress-Up-Learning-Games-/** on any phone, tablet or computer. On an iPad or iPhone, tap Share → Add to Home Screen to give it its own icon.

The link works once GitHub Pages is on for this repo (Settings → Pages → deploy from `main`, root folder). The game is a single HTML file with no build step, so you can also open `index.html` straight in a browser.
